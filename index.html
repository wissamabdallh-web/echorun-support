Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Move your character, collect the key, avoid ghosts, and reach the safe house before time runs out.

Why can't I hear music?

Check the in-game sound setting and your device's volume.

How does the shield work?

The shield protects you from one ghost hit.

How do I throw water?

Collect the water bottle, then press the THROW WATER button to slow the ghost.
